> Step 5 of [Build a page](../../../../.claude/skills/elephants-design/references/build-page.md). Observed locally on 2026-11-15 on `mockup.html`, generated by `build.py` from `sample/` (real responses of our local API, cut 2026-11, data as of 7 nov 2026). Guide v1.1.

## What the mockup shows

14 screens (iteration 2 below): 12 at phone width (390 px) and 2 on desktop (1280 px), drawn from real geometry and real figures.

- Phone: Colombia; Arauca department (58 % of its contracts are department-level only); Arauca municipality; Cacahual, Guainía («Sin datos»); Villa de San Diego de Ubaté (longest name); San Andrés (longest department name); search with repeated names («la uni» → 4 × La Unión); search with no results; invalid link `/mapa/99999`; loading; 503 «datos sin publicar»; geometry error with the rest of the page still working.
- Desktop: Colombia with the table open; Arauca municipality.

## Iteration 2 (2026-11-15, after the user's review)

The user found two problems: the zoom controls covered the map, and San Andrés did not look like the islands and sat on top of the mainland. They asked for world-class detail. Changes:

- **Controls under the map** (`el-map-tools`), never over the shapes. «Ver todo el país» appears only at department level, where there is somewhere to go back to. − and + are one group of two 44 px buttons.
- **San Andrés: the root cause was the geometry, not the drawing.** At 0.4 % simplification each island reached the API as a 6-point polygon. `scripts/geometria-mgn.mjs` now keeps 10 % for department 88 only: 135 points for San Andrés, and 95 + 25 for Providencia y Santa Catalina. The file grew 1.6 KB and stays within budget (342,200 B; 73,901 B with Brotli). The front would have inherited the same defect.
- **Inset** placed in the Caribbean corner, computed so it never touches land (82 × 91 px at phone width). It shows both islands side by side at the same scale and links to the department. A note under the map names it.
- **Archipelago at department level:** the islands are drawn side by side with their names, and a note says the real distance (about 100 km, measured from the geometry).
- **Counts as rows with their share** (`el-counts-rows`): «En un municipio 6.771.167 · 90,8 %». At 390 px the three counts no longer break into 1 + 2, and Arauca shows its 41,8 / 58,2 % split at a glance.
- **«Sin datos»** in the figure is smaller and muted (`value-empty`), so it does not read as a number.
- The guide's specimen (`source.html`) shows the same layout, and its rules text was updated.

Re-checked after iteration 2: no overflow at 320, 390, 768 or 1280 px; 0 controls without a name; `impeccable detect` 0 anti-patterns on the mockup and on `preview.html`. It flagged 22 `cramped-padding` on the new count rows, fixed with a 4 px inset. Light and dark screenshots reviewed.

## Iteration 3 (2026-11-15, after the user's review)

The user pointed out that the method note is a clarification of a figure, so it belongs next to that figure, not in a separate block. Changes:

- **New guide component, Data help (`el-toggletip-btn`, `el-toggletip`).** An (i) button sits right after the label it explains. It uses the toggletip pattern, not a hover tooltip, because the guide rule is that nothing depends on hover. It opens on tap, click or Enter, and closes with Esc or a second tap. The text appears in a `role="status"` region under the label and pushes the content down, so it never covers the number.
- **The text depends on the level:**
  - Colombia: on the new «Dónde quedó cada contrato» title above the breakdown, which is what the note explains.
  - Department: on «Contratos ubicados en …». It says the figure adds the municipal and department-level contracts.
  - Municipality: on «Contratos ubicados en el municipio». It says contracts that cover several municipalities are not included.
- The separate method block was removed from all three panels. The mockup shows the help open on Arauca (department and municipality) and on the desktop municipality screen. The toggletips in the mockup work, so they can be tried.
- Checked:
  - with all 12 open, no overflow at 320, 390, 768 or 1280 px;
  - each button is 44 × 44 px with an accessible name;
  - Esc closes the help;
  - `impeccable detect` finds 0 anti-patterns on the mockup and on `preview.html`.
- Removed «Quitar selección» from the municipality panel. It was not in the spec, and the breadcrumb and «Ver <departamento>» already go back to the department.
- **Sources once** (raised by the user). Each item now appears in one place: the date in the dek, the source on the key figure (guide rule), and licences plus the publication date in the footer. The footer had the Marco Geoestadístico twice (list and «Límites»), and table captions repeated the source and date. Per screen, the source line now appears in 2 places instead of 4 (figure and footer); the date in 3 instead of 5 (dek, figure and footer).
- **The component needs the user's approval** (spec §8: approval per component). It is in the guide v1.1 diff with a specimen (`preview.html#ayuda`).

## Iteration 7 (2026-11-15): independent review

An agent without my context reviewed the 14 screens in light and dark against the brief, the guide and the spec. It reported 13 defects, 5 improvements and 2 questions. What changed:

- **Map contrast** (spec §7 promises 3:1 boundaries; measured 1.3:1). Neutral, «Sin datos» and context shapes now have `text-muted` boundaries (6.3:1 light, 7.9:1 dark). A general `.el-map path` rule was overriding them on specificity; fixed. The legend key has the same outline.
- **Selection.** A surface-colour halo plus a 3 px text-colour outline (`el-sel-halo`, `el-sel`). The department border is now 1.5 px, so it no longer looks like the selection. The selected territory is named on the map, inside it when it has room and below it when it is small (`el-map-label-on`, `el-map-label-below`). Bogotá is named inside Cundinamarca.
- **One frame proportion** for every department view (height/width 1–1.25), so the panel does not jump between levels. Cropped views get a 1 px rule (`el-map-canvas-crop`).
- **Loading:** neutral dek; a visibly disabled search with «La búsqueda se activa cuando cargue el mapa»; a plain frame instead of a dashed box.
- **503** is no longer a dead end: it points to SECOP II on datos.gov.co. New screens: network/500 error, Guainía (department with «Sin datos»), Bogotá, D.C. (department with one municipality). 17 screens in total.
- The figure label depends on the territory type: «en el área», «en la isla» or «en el municipio».
- «Alejar» is disabled at the minimum zoom.
- **Screen readers:** no `aria-live` on whole panels. One short hidden announcement per level (spec §7), and an `h2` that can take focus. Each map has a `<desc>` naming the selection.
- «Ver en una tabla» has a chevron that turns when open (`el-disclosure`). Table headers are shorter. Tables that still scroll at 320 px show an edge shadow (`el-table-scroll`).
- The breadcrumb separator is attached to the previous item, so a line never starts with «›».
- Count rows: the whole list decides together (container query on the list). One line when it is wider than 340 px; below that, label above and number with share right-aligned. Previously one row in a list could wrap while the others did not.
- The toggletip uses spacing tokens only and is a rule, not a tinted card.
- **Legend:** in the right column on desktop (spec §5); «Seleccionado» only when something is selected.
- The country hint is above the map.

## Checks

- **Contrast.** The guide's `build.py` passes in light and dark (v1.1 adds informational rows for `line` and `surface-subtle`). The map uses no colour to encode quantity: the fill is neutral, «Sin datos» is the grey hatch plus the words «Sin datos», and selection is a strong outline. Nothing depends on colour alone.
- **Overflow.** Each screen re-laid out inside the page at 320, 390, 768 and 1280 px (container queries). No element passes the right edge at any width. The tables scroll inside `.table-wrap` at 320 px, which is the intended behaviour.
  - Fixed during the check: at 320 px the desktop grid had no `minmax(0,1fr)` column, so the heading overflowed. Fixed.
- **Keyboard and names** (Arauca municipality screen). The tab order follows the reading order: search → breadcrumb → zoom (Acercar, Alejar, Ver todo el país) → panel links → Quitar selección → table → sources. Every button has an accessible name (0 without one). Every map `svg` has `role="img"` and an `aria-label` and `title` (0 without). Headings: h1 → h2 «Mapa» (visually hidden) → h3 «Cómo leer el mapa» → h2 territory → h2 «Fuentes y licencias».
  - The search follows the WAI-ARIA combobox pattern: `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`, `aria-activedescendant`, and a listbox. The no-results message is `role="status"`.
- **`impeccable detect`** (run with telemetry off for the run): **0 anti-patterns.**
  - One advisory, `repeating-stripes-gradient`. This is the grey hatch for missing data, already accepted in the guide.
  - Fixed during the check: 11 `skipped-heading` (h1 → h3). I added the hidden h2 «Mapa».
- **Copy** (tone rules from the brief):
  - The copy never uses «corrupción», «robo» or «sospechoso», and no natural person appears.
  - It says «contratos ubicados en», never «ejecutados en», and always gives the window (2021–2026) and the source and date.
  - «Sin datos» never appears as 0.
  - Repeated names always come with their department.
  - Department-level contracts are explained on the municipality screen («Además, N contratos del departamento… no se pueden ubicar en un solo municipio»).
  - The loading and 503 states use the neutral heading «Contratación pública en Colombia, departamento por departamento» because they have no figures yet.
- **Visual review** (screenshots, light and dark). Fixes made during the review:
  - zoom buttons of 44 × 44 px;
  - the search icon anchored to the input (`el-search-box`, added to components v1.1);
  - wide departments (Arauca) given room so the controls do not cover the shape;
  - the San Andrés inset widened so its label fits, with the islands outlined so they are visible at that size;
  - the breadcrumb hidden at the Colombia level, where it repeats the panel heading;
  - mobile order per the spec: search, breadcrumb, map, panel, table.

## 10-second test (spec §1), answered honestly

1. **Map of Colombia by department, and I can go into mine.** Mostly yes: the outline of Colombia is recognisable, and the prompt «Toca un departamento o búscalo para ver sus municipios» says it. Weak point: on the phone the map comes after the heading and the search box, so the map is partly below the first screen on small phones.
2. **How to find my place.** Yes: the search box is the first control, with an example (Arauca, Ubaté).
3. **Where the data comes from and how current it is.** Yes: the subtitle says «Datos de SECOP al 7 de noviembre de 2026», and every figure repeats the source and date.

Not tested with real people: this is my reading, not a user test.

## Limits

- Interaction is not prototyped: hover label on desktop, zoom, map selection, and the search filtering behaviour are specified in `spec.md` but drawn only as states.
- The mockup draws the geometry with a simple equirectangular projection. The front end will use the projection the spec decides.
- `design:accessibility-review` and `design:ux-copy` were not run as separate skills. The checks above cover their points for this page.
- Changes to the guide made during validation (`el-search-box`, zoom sizes, inset width) are in the uncommitted v1.1 diff. They need the user's approval with this page.
