Copy items (#12):
- «Toca» on desktop: use «Elige» or «Toca o haz clic».
- The window is worded three ways: «entre 2021 y 2026» (headline), «entre 2021 y el 7 nov 2026» (panel), «2021–2026» (captions). 2026 is partial.
- «solo en un departamento» and «sin lugar identificado» are unexplained.
- «Contratos públicos» above «356.935 contratos…» repeats the noun.
- Bogotá:
  - its dek says «Toca un municipio» although it has only one;
  - the search lists it twice («Departamento» and «Bogotá, D.C.»);
  - 1.190.373 appears twice.

## False positives

- CLI `low-contrast` ×3 (#000000 on #2b2312, #f2c46d on #fff, #a6b4bf on #fff). The static scan pairs tokens across themes. Rendered text is at least 6.3:1 in light and 8.3:1 in dark.
- `repeating-stripes-gradient`: the «Sin datos» hatch, intended by the guide.
- `kicker-above-heading`: the accepted Round 05 editorial pattern. Kept.

## What works (keep)

- **Copy discipline.** Every figure has a noun, source and cut date. «Sin datos» never appears as 0. The legend says that grey does not mean «sin problemas».
- **The desktop municipality panel.** Name, code, rule, figure, sentence and source form a unit that can be screenshot and shared.
- **Search.** It ignores accents and shows «Municipio, Departamento» (Granada ×3). Controls are 44 px. The empty state is useful.
- **No horizontal overflow** at 320, 390, 768 or 1280 px.

## Top 3 changes

1. Make the mockup standalone (#1).
2. Make the map visible with existing tokens: an outline plus hover and focus (#2).
3. On a phone, put the selected territory under the map and move to it (#3).

## Decisions needed

- **Hatch weight vs the guide's 3:1 hatch rule (#4).** Keep the hatch as it is and rely on #2 to restore the order (recommended), or open a guide change?
- **Place labels (#5).** Recommended: label the selected territory, the inset and Bogotá. Department labels on the desktop national view: yes or no?
- **H1 on a municipality URL (#3).** Municipality name as H1 with the department as kicker? This changes the spec's hierarchy.

**Resolved by the user (2026-11-15):**
- Hatch: unchanged.
- Labels: the selected territory, the inset and Bogotá, D.C.; no department labels.
- `h1` on a municipality: the municipality's name.

Findings #2–#13 were carried into the [map spec](../pages/map/spec.md) (decisions 9–11b, layout and copy). #1 is a requirement for the step-4 mockup.
