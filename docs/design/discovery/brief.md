# Design brief

Date: 2026-11-08. Answered by: Jouler (EKOU), confirmed in the design discovery session. Yardstick for design audits and rounds (see `.claude/skills/elephants-design`).

## Audiences and first tasks

- **General public** (default experience): the first thing they achieve is the **national overview** — where in Colombia signals concentrate — and then they explore. Source: team.
- **Interested public** (journalists, oversight groups, students, community leaders): **compare and verify** territories, entities and contractors with exact figures, source and date, so they can cite or check a story. Source: team.
- Two audiences and their views (simple default view, full detail view) come from the project `README.md`.

## Devices and reading level

- **Mobile first**: mid-range phones, limited mobile data, content often arriving shared through WhatsApp or social networks. Desktop matters mostly for journalists. Source: team.
- Readers without technical knowledge or with difficulty interpreting public information (`README.md`): everyday language, no procurement jargon.

## Risk voice

- Allowed: **direct and mobilising** — state the fact with force and a number ("Esta obra lleva 2 años detenida", "Solo una empresa se presentó"). Source: team.
- Avoid: "corrupción", "robo", "sospechoso" and similar verdicts or adjectives; names of natural persons (TASK-08); implying that "low risk" means no irregularities. The force comes from the fact and the figure, not from a qualifier. Source: `visual-direction.md`; boundary confirmed by the team.

## Trust signals

- Visual world: **data journalism** — a serious outlet's interactive special. A headline that says something, the map as protagonist, large figures with source and date. Independent and mobilising, not a government portal and not a SaaS dashboard. Source: team. Confirmed 2026-11-08 by selecting the editorial Round 05 over the cleaner Round 06.
- Every figure travels with its unit, source, period and update date; coverage is shown separately (`guide/design-system.md`).

## Constraints

- Accepted colour roles: blue lower risk, yellow intermediate, warm red higher, grey hatch for insufficient data; no invented thresholds until TASK-07 (`visual-direction.md`).
- WCAG AA text contrast, 44 px controls, colour never alone (`guide/design-system.md`).
- Free services only; restrained EKOU brand presence; product copy in Spanish (Colombia), repository documents in English.

## Success criteria

- **10-second test**: a person without experience looks at the national screen on a phone and within 10 seconds can say where signals concentrate, what the colour means and that it is not an accusation. Source: team.
- Secondary (assumed, confirmed): a screenshot of a figure or the map is understandable on its own when shared — figure, meaning and source legible in the capture.

## Open questions

- Numeric risk categories, labels and the contract-versus-work unit (TASK-07).
- Display of natural persons (TASK-08).
- Typeface: Atkinson Hyperlegible Next versus system font (Round 04).
